# yt-dlp video only no audio


https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp/issues/10189

 I ran into the same problem but all of the formats with the resolution I wanted was marked "video-only". I found out I could make yt-dlp download a video stream and an audio stream and merge it automatically by doing something like this:

yt-dlp -f 397+140 [URL]

397 is the video-only stream, while 140 is the audio-only stream.

Hope that helps you in the future!
